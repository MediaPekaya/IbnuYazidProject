# Video Content Calendar

## Base prompt
> Create a 30-day video content calendar for [niche/brand] on [platforms]: date, format (short/long/live), topic, hook, CTA and goal. Mix educational, entertaining and selling content.

## How to apply
- Use `templates/video/content-calendar.md`; follow ~60% value, 25% engagement, 15% selling.
- Reuse one long video into multiple shorts; mark repurposing links.
- Include batch-filming days to reduce production load.
- Reply in the user's language; end with the first 3 videos to produce this week.
