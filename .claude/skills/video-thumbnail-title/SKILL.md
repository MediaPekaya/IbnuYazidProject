# Video Title & Thumbnail

## Base prompt
> Give 10 title options, 3 thumbnail concepts (text, image, colours, expression) and an SEO description with keywords and hashtags for a video about [topic].

## How to apply
- Titles under ~60 characters, curiosity or benefit-led, never misleading.
- Thumbnail: max 3-5 words, one focal subject, high contrast; specify layout and colour palette.
- Provide A/B test pairs and the strongest pick with a one-line reason.
- Reply in the user's language; end with a publishing checklist.
